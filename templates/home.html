BMCC Accessible Education

Inclusive learning support for BMCC students in one study platform.

StudyBridge helps BMCC students from different educational, linguistic, and cultural backgrounds access learning support in one place. Students can log in, register for classes, explore course materials, use book and YouTube resources, join study groups, request academic help, and book free tutoring appointments.

  • Study groups that encourage peer connection
  • Free tutoring appointment booking
  • One simple platform for inclusive academic access
